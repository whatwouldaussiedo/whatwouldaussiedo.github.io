Privacy

What Would Aussie Do? is a single-player strategy game. You do not need an account, and the game does not ask for your name, email, location or contacts.

Your game

Your saved game, language and sound settings are kept only on your own device (in the iPhone app's storage, or your browser's local storage). They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, removes them.

Web preview: visit counts

The free web preview counts page visits, games started, games finished and the grades reached, using GoatCounter. It uses no cookies and does not keep your IP address; it records only the country a visit comes from, worked out at the moment of the visit, plus your browser type and screen size. Nothing it records can identify you. The iPhone app counts nothing.

Feedback

"Tell us what you think" opens a Google Form. It does not collect your email address. It is filled in with the game version, the device type and where you are in the game, so a bug report says enough to find the bug. Only send what you are happy to share; Google's privacy policy applies to the form.

Changes and contact

If this changes, this page will say so. Questions: use the feedback form in the game.

Support

What Would Aussie Do? is a single-player strategy game for iPhone. Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Get in touch:

  • Email: bomapphelp@gmail.com (we usually reply within a few days)
  • In the game: ☰ → Feedback, which fills in your game version and where you are in the game for you

Common questions

Where is my game saved? On your iPhone only. The game saves as you play; choose Continue on the title screen to carry on. Deleting the app deletes the saved game.

How do I change the language, sound or vibration? Title screen or ☰ menu → Settings.

How do I learn to play? Settings → How to play runs the tutorial again; Rules explains everything in short sections.

Why does my money keep falling? Tap the money figure at the top of the screen: it lists every daily cost and income. Trade stops when a great power's attitude to Australia falls below 5, so keep up relations through Diplomacy.

Is this about real events? No. It is a work of fiction. Every country in the game plays by the same rules.

  • 電郵：bomapphelp@gmail.com（一般會在數日內回覆）
  • 遊戲內：☰ → 意見回饋，會自動填上遊戲版本和你玩到哪裡

常見問題

遊戲存在哪裡？只存在你的 iPhone。遊戲會邊玩邊自動儲存，在開始畫面按繼續遊戲即可。刪除 app 會一併刪除存檔。

點樣改語言、音效或者震動？開始畫面或 ☰ 選單 → 設定。

點樣學玩？設定 → 新手教學可以重溫教學；規則分段簡單說明所有玩法。

點解啲錢不停跌？點一下畫面頂部的國庫數字，會列出每日所有收支。大國對澳態度跌穿 5 就會停止貿易，記得透過「外交」維持關係。

遊戲內容是真實的嗎？不是。遊戲純屬虛構，遊戲中所有國家都按同一套規則運作。
